# Customer interview script (The Mom Test)

> Interviews are STUBBED: the founder runs them with real people. VentureGate never writes interview answers or customer quotes.

**Who to interview:** past_behavior

## What we want to learn

- Understand the founder's past experience with similar problems
- Identify the founder's current workarounds for the problem
- Determine what the founder already spends time or money on

## Questions

1. What was the problem you faced last time that required a solution?  
   _Why:_ To understand the founder's experience with similar problems.
2. How do you currently handle the problem you faced last time?  
   _Why:_ To identify the founder's current workarounds for the problem.
3. What do you already spend time or money on to address the problem?  
   _Why:_ To determine what the founder already spends time or money on to address the problem.
4. What was the outcome of the solution you implemented for the problem?  
   _Why:_ To understand the effectiveness of the founder's current solution.
5. What was the cost or time investment for the solution?  
   _Why:_ To assess the founder's current resource allocation for the problem.
6. What are the limitations or challenges of the solution?  
   _Why:_ To identify potential constraints or challenges the founder faces.
7. What is the founder's current approach to solving the problem?  
   _Why:_ To understand the founder's current strategy for addressing the problem.
8. What is the founder's perception of the problem's severity?  
   _Why:_ To gauge the founder's perception of the problem's importance.
9. What is the founder's opinion on the effectiveness of the solution?  
   _Why:_ To evaluate the founder's satisfaction with the current solution.
10. What is the founder's plan for future improvements?  
   _Why:_ To identify potential future enhancements or solutions.
11. What is the founder's current focus or priority?  
   _Why:_ To understand the founder's current priorities and focus.

## Avoid

- Hypothetical or future questions
- Questions that require the founder to provide an answer
