# Customer interview script (The Mom Test)

> Interviews are STUBBED: the founder runs them with real people. VentureGate never writes interview answers or customer quotes.

**Who to interview:** Customer Interview Script - The Mom Test

## What we want to learn

- Understand the founder's past experiences with similar problems
- Identify the founder's current workarounds for their pain points
- Discover what the founder already spends time or money on
- Evaluate the founder's current approach to solving their problem

## Questions

1. What was the last time you faced a problem that required a solution?  
   _Why:_ To understand the founder's past experiences with similar problems.
2. How do you currently handle the problem you're trying to solve?  
   _Why:_ To identify the founder's current workarounds for their pain points.
3. What do you already spend time or money on to address this problem?  
   _Why:_ To discover what the founder already spends time or money on to address their problem.
4. What is the current approach you take to solve this problem?  
   _Why:_ To evaluate the founder's current approach to solving their problem.

## Avoid

- Hypothetical questions about future behavior or decisions
- Questions that ask for opinions or judgments
